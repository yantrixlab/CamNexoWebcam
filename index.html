Features

Everything a good webcam should do

Phone cameras are far better than built-in laptop webcams. CamNexo is the simple, quick way to use one.

Finds your computer by itself

Open the app on both devices on the same Wi-Fi and they connect. It remembers your computer and reconnects automatically.

Resolutions up to 4K

Pick any size your phone supports, from 360p to 4K, or choose Auto and let CamNexo adapt to your connection. Change it live from your computer.

Fixed or automatic framing

Keep every picture a steady landscape or portrait frame whichever way the phone is held, or let it follow its cell. Flip any camera for upside-down mounts.

Low delay, hardware encoded

Your phone encodes video with its own hardware H.264 encoder, so the stream stays smooth and your phone stays cool.

Keeps going in the background

Switch to another app on your phone and the camera keeps streaming. Stop it any time from the notification.

No account, no tracking

There is nothing to sign up for. CamNexo has no analytics and no ads, and your video stays between your own devices.

Several phones at once

Connect up to eight phones. Each one shows up in the app and gets its own place in the picture that Zoom, Meet and OBS see as one camera.

Layouts you control

Pick Side by side, Main + 2, a grid and more, or split the frame yourself and drag the dividers. Show or hide any camera, or keep every picture landscape or portrait.

Front or back, live

Switch between your phone's front and back camera while streaming. The call carries on and the picture changes in a moment.

Blur or replace your background

Hide a messy room with a soft blur, or put a picture behind you. It is applied before the picture reaches Zoom, Meet, Teams or OBS, so it works in all of them.

Colour looks

Pick Warm, Vivid, Soft, Cinema or Mono, or set brightness, contrast, colour and warmth yourself. Each camera can have its own look.

Worked out on your computer

Effects are calculated on your own computer from the picture your phone sends. Nothing about your picture is uploaded anywhere.

How it works

Three steps, about a minute

Nothing to configure. If your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi, you are nearly there.

Install on both

Put the CamNexo app on your phone and the desktop app on your computer. The desktop app sets up the virtual camera for you.

Tap Connect

Your computer appears in the phone app's nearby list. Tap it once. Next time it connects on its own.

Pick “CamNexo”

In Zoom, Meet, Teams, OBS or any app that uses a camera, choose CamNexo from the camera list.

Quality

Choose the size that suits the call

The list adapts to your phone. Sizes your camera or encoder cannot do are never offered.

Privacy first

Your video goes from your phone to your computer. Nowhere else.

CamNexo has no servers that touch your video. The phone connects straight to your computer across your own network.

  • No account and no personal details to give us.
  • No analytics or ad trackers in the apps or on this website.
  • Camera only while you stream. A notification and your phone's camera indicator show when it is on.

FAQ

Good questions

Does the background effect work in Zoom, Meet and Teams?

Yes. The effect is applied inside CamNexo, before the picture becomes the CamNexo camera, so any app that uses the camera shows it. You do not need to turn on a second effect in the video app. The cut-out works best with even light and one person in front of the camera.

Do the effects need a powerful computer?

A normal recent computer is enough: on a mid-range desktop processor one camera with a blurred background uses a small share of the processor. Effects use the processor, not the graphics card. If the picture stutters with several phones, lower the size of each phone from the Cameras panel.

Can I use more than one phone?

Yes, up to eight at once. Open CamNexo on each phone and connect it to the same computer. Choose a layout in the desktop app, or split the frame yourself, and apps see one camera called CamNexo showing the combined picture. You can hide any phone without disconnecting it.

Which apps does it work with?

CamNexo appears as a standard camera called “CamNexo”, so it works with apps that accept a normal webcam: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, OBS, Discord and browsers. On Windows 11 the Camera app shows it too.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account. Your phone and computer only need to be on the same local network. They do not need internet access to talk to each other, although your video call app will.

Is the connection encrypted?

Not yet. Today the video travels unencrypted across your local network, so use CamNexo on a network you trust, such as your home or office Wi-Fi, and not on public Wi-Fi. Pairing and encryption are the next security work on our list. See the safety notes.

How good is the picture?

That depends on your phone. CamNexo lists the sizes your phone's camera and hardware encoder support, up to 4K on phones that can do it. Wi-Fi quality matters too, and Auto lowers the size if your connection struggles.

Does it keep streaming if I use another app?

Yes. While streaming, CamNexo shows a notification and keeps the camera running in the background. Stop it from the app or the notification. Some phone makers limit background apps aggressively; if streaming stops, exclude CamNexo from battery optimisation.

What does it cost?

Early-access builds are free. Pricing for the finished product has not been announced.

Stop squinting at a grainy webcam.

Use the camera in your pocket. Set it up once, and it is there every time you join a call.
