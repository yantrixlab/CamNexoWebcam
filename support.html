Quick start

  1. Connect your phone and computer to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open the CamNexo desktop app on your computer. Allow it through the firewall on private networks if Windows asks.
  3. Open the CamNexo app on your phone and allow camera access. Your computer appears under “Computers nearby”. Tap Connect.
  4. In your video app, choose CamNexo as the camera.

Choose the resolution and framing from the desktop app's top bar. The phone follows your choice.

Using several phones

  1. Open CamNexo on each phone and tap Connect on the same computer.
  2. In the desktop app, open Cameras to see every phone. Use the eye button to show or hide one, the size menu to change its quality, and the flip button for a phone mounted upside down.
  3. Open Layout. Auto picks a layout from the number of cameras. Or pick a template such as Side by side or Main + 2.
  4. For your own layout, choose Edit layout. Use the buttons on each cell to split it, remove it, or choose its camera, and drag the dividers to resize.
  5. Picture shape keeps every camera in a landscape or portrait frame, whatever the cell or the way the phone is held. Auto lets each cell decide.

Zoom, Meet, Teams and OBS see one camera, CamNexo, showing exactly the picture in the desktop window. Many phones at once need a good Wi-Fi network; if the picture stutters, lower each phone's size from the Cameras panel.

Background and looks

  1. Click Effects in the top bar. With several phones, pick the camera at the top of the panel. The first time, the panel offers a one-time Download (about 27 MB) of the files that blur and replace the background; it needs an internet connection once and is saved on your computer. Colour looks work without it.
  2. Under Background choose Blur and set the strength, or Picture to pick one of the ready-made backgrounds or your own image.
  3. Under Look pick a style, and use Adjust for brightness, contrast, colour and warmth. Apply to all cameras copies the settings.

Effects are applied to the picture every app receives, so Zoom, Meet, Teams and OBS show them too. For the cleanest edge use even light, sit in front of a plainer wall, and keep one person in the picture. If the effect cannot run, the panel says so and the colour looks still work; use Download the effects files again in the panel to repair it.

Chrome or Windows warns about the installer

You may see “Suspicious download blocked” in Chrome, or “Windows protected your PC” from Microsoft Defender SmartScreen. This happens because the early-access installer is not code-signed yet and few people have downloaded it, so neither Chrome nor Windows recognizes it. It does not mean the file has been found to be harmful. We are working on getting the installer signed, and the warnings fade as it becomes better known.

Check the file first

Download only from this website. Then open PowerShell in your Downloads folder and run Get-FileHash .CamNexo-Setup-*.exe. The result should match the SHA-256 you get by clicking Verify download on the Windows card on the home page. If it does not match, do not run it, and tell us.

If you decide to install it

  • Chrome: open the downloads list, find CamNexo-Setup, and choose Keep (or Keep anyway).
  • Windows SmartScreen: click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Windows then asks for administrator permission, because the installer sets up the virtual camera.

If you would rather wait for a signed version, that is a good choice. Email and we will tell you when it is ready.

My computer does not appear on the phone

  • Check both devices are on the same network, and the same Wi-Fi band or router, not a separate guest network.
  • Make sure the desktop app is open and says it is waiting for a phone.
  • Some routers block devices from seeing each other (“client isolation” or “AP isolation”). Turn it off, or use a phone hotspot with the computer joined to it.
  • As a workaround, tap Enter address manually in the phone app and type the computer's address shown in the desktop app's details panel.
  • Allow CamNexo through the Windows firewall on private networks. The receiver uses TCP port 7878.

“CamNexo” is not in my video app's camera list

  • Close and reopen the video app after the desktop app has started.
  • The desktop app shows whether the camera is ready. If it asks for setup, follow its instructions (administrator permission is needed once).
  • Check Windows Settings, Privacy and security, Camera, and make sure apps are allowed to use the camera.
  • If Windows' camera service stopped, starting the desktop app again restarts it.

The picture is black or frozen

  • Make sure the phone app says Streaming and the desktop app shows a live picture.
  • Lower the resolution from the desktop app, or switch to Auto, especially at 4K.
  • Move closer to the router or use a 5 GHz network.
  • Stop and start streaming on the phone.

Streaming stops when I use other apps

CamNexo should keep streaming in the background and show a notification. If it stops, open your phone's settings for CamNexo, allow notifications, and set battery use to “Unrestricted” (the wording varies by phone maker).

The picture is sideways or cropped

In the desktop app, Auto fits the whole picture and the frame follows how you hold the phone. Fixed Landscape and Fixed Portrait keep one frame shape and crop the picture to fill it. Use the flip button if your phone is mounted upside down.
